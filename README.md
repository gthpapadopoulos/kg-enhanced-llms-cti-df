# Knowledge-Graph-Enhanced LLMs for Cyber Threat Intelligence and Digital Forensics: the 53 studies

**Browse the list:** https://gthpapadopoulos.github.io/kg-enhanced-llms-cti-df/

This repository lists the 53 studies analysed in the paper

> C. Sardianos, V. Efthymiou, I. Varlamis, T. Lagkas, P. Sarigiannidis, and G. Th. Papadopoulos, "Knowledge-Graph-Enhanced LLMs for Cyber Threat Intelligence and Digital Forensics: A Meta-Analysis," manuscript, 2026.

The web page (`index.html`) shows each study as printed in its PDF (title, authors, venue, DOI or arXiv link), its domain and tier, the contrast types and study-level gain ratio, the reporting audit (same LLM in all arms, generic KG arm, variance, hallucination, unanswerable questions, cost, code), and three validity concerns (ground truth derived from the graph, fewer than 50 test items, no same-LLM contrast). Each row opens to the task, the LLMs, the test set, per-category results, and multi-LLM codes. The page can be searched, filtered by domain, and sorted by any column.
